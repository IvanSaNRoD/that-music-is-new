# Brief — Result screen (desktop)

Confirmed 2026-11-13 via `/impeccable shape`. Builds on the desktop home (DESIGN.md).

1. **Job & audience** — A music lover just rolled a genre and wants to *hear it now* to decide if it hooks them. Mode: Operate (show the genre, pick/play tracks, clear exits).
2. **Outcome** — Primary action: play a track. First track played adds the genre to History. Success: a track plays within seconds; "Listen more on Spotify" if it hooks. Real content (PRD): genre name, track count of its "The Sound of X" playlist, up to 10 tracks (title, artists), Spotify playlist link, one Spotify Embed player. No origin/era (Spotify doesn't provide it).
3. **Direction** — *Revised 2026-11-13* (Claude Design handoff `docs/claude-design/design_handoff_turntable`, option 1b, adapted to DESIGN.md): result takes the **full width** (Hero + crate + History column replaced). Left: a **turntable** (Card Stock plinth, 1.5px Sumi outline, 16px radius; `<Vinyl detailed>` whose label = the genre's sleeve-tone hole colour; Aged Paper tonearm + pivot outlined in Sumi; "33⅓" in Space Mono) with the compact player below. Right: genre name (Inter Tight 64), Space Mono meta, 10 tracks as pill rows (two columns of five ≥1280px), then **"Your finds"** (last 5 History entries as vinyl pills, click → that genre). Focal moment: the record lands on the platter (0.7s, lift curve); it spins while loading or playing, and the arm drops onto the grooves when a track plays (24°, 0.7s). Not adopted from the handoff: Organic theme (Caprasimo/Figtree, terracotta), origin/era, durations, own ⏮ ▶ ⏭ controls (playback lives in the Spotify Embed).
4. **Scope** — Desktop only, coded with placeholder data; player is a placeholder until E4.3 (then the turntable follows the embed's `playback_update`). Untouched: header, steps band. History management (remove, clear, undo) only on home. Anti-goals: no Spotify-page look, no dashboard table.
5. **States** — Loading: record spins with the arm up + skeleton track rows; while the route resolves, an empty dashed platter. Result: 1–10 tracks; genre names up to ~40 chars. Error: plain message + Try again, empty platter. 429 → "Spotify is busy — try again in N s" (announced once via the page live region, then "You can try again now"), empty platter. Empty (no playable tracks): message only (the top Roll again is the action), still record. Listen more on Spotify stays visible in every settled state. Geometry is identical across states (player slot always reserved, skeleton rows = real row height).
6. **Interaction** — Randomize / Space → straight to result. Play on open sleeve or History row → that genre's result. Track click → player + "now playing" row. Roll again (stays in result), Back (→ hero + crate), Listen more on Spotify (new tab). Result arrival announced via aria-live; focus moves to the genre heading (not a track button, so Space still means Roll again). Reading/DOM order: Back → title, tracks, finds → player (placed under the deck by the grid). Busy (429): the countdown is announced once and again when retry is possible; Roll again (top) stays available while waiting.
7. **Open** — Final player look (Spotify iframe). Clear-all confirm already inline.
