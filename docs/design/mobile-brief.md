# Brief — Mobile + touch (home, genre page)

Confirmed 2026-11-15 via `/impeccable shape` (issue #42). Extends DESIGN.md; no new visual world, no new tokens unless a size step is missing.

1. **Job & audience** — The same music lovers in an exploration moment on a phone (one hand, often standing or on the sofa), or on a tablet. Mode: Operate. They want one tap to a genre and one tap to hear a track.
2. **Outcome** — Home: Randomize reachable in the first viewport; a sleeve opens and plays with taps. Genre page: the first tracks are visible without scrolling at 390×844; play, Roll again and the player are always within thumb reach. Success = no hover-only action, no target under 44px, no horizontal overflow at 320px.
3. **Direction** — One layout below 1024px (no separate tablet design), inside a centred max-width column (≈640–720px) so tablets don't stretch it. Touch behaviour follows the input, not the width: `(hover: none), (pointer: coarse)` drives target sizes and hover replacements, so iPad landscape (desktop layout, touch) is covered too.
   - **Home:** same order as desktop, compacted: header → hero (56px display + Randomize) → crate (8 tappable sleeves) → History → steps (vertical connectors). Nothing fixed on screen.
   - **Genre page:** "Back to the crate" → a **mini turntable (~120px) beside the genre title + meta** → 10 tracks in one column → Pencil Rule → "Your finds" as one horizontally scrollable row. The mini deck keeps the genre tone, landing, spin and arm (same geometry, scaled).
   - **Bottom bar (genre page only):** fixed at the bottom: compact player slot (now playing / "Dig deeper on Spotify ↗") + Roll again (secondary, ≥44px). Safe-area inset respected; page content padded so the last finds stay reachable. Replaces the top-right Roll again below 1024px; "Listen more on Spotify" stays with the title as a ≥44px link.
4. **Scope** — Home + genre page incl. loading, error, busy (429), empty, not-found, below 1024px, plus touch rules at every width. Untouched: desktop ≥1024px layouts (only touch-target tweaks under the touch query), copy, data, tokens' colours. Anti-goals: no hamburger/nav drawer, no app-store look, no second fixed bar on the home, no new hues.
5. **States & ranges** — Genre names 5–40 chars (genre title needs a mobile step ≈40–44px, ≤3 lines, never overflows at 320px). 1–10 tracks; truncated titles stay legible. History 0–5. Error/busy/empty keep the bottom bar (Roll again is the action; player slot shows nothing playing). Loading: mini deck spinning, skeleton rows.
6. **Interaction** — Touch replacements: History row ✕ always visible (no hover reveal); "Your finds" lift and track hover ▶ off on touch (the cued row 01 ▶ stays); Space shortcut + kbd hint hidden on touch. All page actions ≥44px on touch. Reading/DOM order unchanged (player last; the bottom bar is placed visually, not moved in the DOM). Focus on arrival = title, as on desktop. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off sleeve rise, deck motion and finds roll-in.
7. **Constraints / open** — Verify at 320, 390, 768, 834, 1024 (touch). Final player = Spotify Embed (E4.3): its compact height must fit the bar; until then the placeholder sizes it. Builder must not invent: a new breakpoint beyond 1024, a sticky Randomize, or hiding the steps. Results move into DESIGN.md › Layout (replacing "Open for the mobile/tablet pass").
